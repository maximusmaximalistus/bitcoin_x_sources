# 14_blockstream_cryptofeed — Blockstream CryptoFeed — exchange market data

The **Blockstream + ICE cryptocurrency market-data feed**, focusing on what data is offered and how it is sourced or used; this is not a blockchain explorer.

## Put here

- Official product descriptions, data-field definitions, venue coverage, market trade/quote records, historical data, order-book depth and VWAP references.
- Licensing/permission notes, source citations, screenshots of authorized examples and comparisons of market-data semantics versus on-chain activity.
- Research on provenance, timestamp granularity, stale data, data subscriptions and known commercial-access boundaries.

## Keep elsewhere / avoid

- Blockstream Explorer blockchain lookup and Bitcoin/Liquid transactions → `../13_blockstream_explorer/`.
- Independent Bitcoin monetary properties and price-independent protocol research → `../05_bitcoin_independent/`.
- Do not upload or republish proprietary feed records without explicit redistribution rights, access tokens, or paid customer data.

## Filing routine (keep the folder flat)

1. Save papers, images, code, links, screenshots, notes and finished posters **directly in this folder**. No required child folders.
2. Give every sourced item a readable filename; include the project/version/date when that affects accuracy. Preserve the official original filename of authentic product photos when possible.
3. For anything used in a public tweet/poster, keep the **primary source URL, checked date, relevant release/network version, and usage/redistribution rights** in a nearby `.md` note or directly below in this README.
4. Treat source documents as evidence, not permission to alter branded artwork. Before publishing, recheck time-sensitive claims and avoid exposing wallet secrets, authentication material or private user data.
