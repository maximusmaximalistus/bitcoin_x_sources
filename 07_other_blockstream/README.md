# 07_other_blockstream — Other Blockstream — cross-product research and infrastructure

A **catch-all for Blockstream items that do not yet have a dedicated numbered folder** and for genuinely cross-product infrastructure stories.

## Put here

- Blockstream Satellite hardware/protocol resources, network relay and availability architecture.
- General Blockstream Research output not better classified as Simplicity, Quantum, Liquid or Bitcoin-independent.
- Wallet interoperability, independent Bitcoin implementations, open-source developer tools and cross-product technical initiatives.
- Future Blockstream projects until they grow large enough for their own category.

## Keep elsewhere / avoid

- Core Lightning → `../10_blockstream_core_lightning/`; Elements → `../11_elements/`; Explorer/API → `../13_blockstream_explorer/`.
- Quantum-focused Blockstream work → `../12_blockstream_quantum/`.

## Filing routine (keep the folder flat)

1. Save papers, images, code, links, screenshots, notes and finished posters **directly in this folder**. No required child folders.
2. Give every sourced item a readable filename; include the project/version/date when that affects accuracy. Preserve the official original filename of authentic product photos when possible.
3. For anything used in a public tweet/poster, keep the **primary source URL, checked date, relevant release/network version, and usage/redistribution rights** in a nearby `.md` note or directly below in this README.
4. Treat source documents as evidence, not permission to alter branded artwork. Before publishing, recheck time-sensitive claims and avoid exposing wallet secrets, authentication material or private user data.
